# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-14)

- Special functions, quadrature and adaptive integration, FFTs, ODE and
  root solvers, interpolation, splines, dense linear algebra, random numbers and
  sampling, with derivative products (autodiff, analytical and hybrid
  candidates) and rigorous enclosures where stated.
- Citation metadata; the roadmap and process notes are replaced by the issue
  tracker. BLAS and LAPACK remain the only external libraries (see README).
